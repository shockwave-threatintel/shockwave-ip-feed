How to use Shockwave

  • Review the methodology and limitations before use.
  • Validate indicators against your own environment.
  • Test in monitoring mode before enforcing blocks.
  • Monitor for false positives and maintain a rollback path.

Understand the limitations

Shockwave aggregates indicators from third-party threat-intelligence sources. Individual entries have not all been independently verified by Shockwave. An indicator is not proof that every connection involving that address is malicious. IPs may be reassigned, shared, or used by legitimate services; false positives remain possible.
